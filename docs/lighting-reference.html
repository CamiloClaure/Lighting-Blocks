Lighting Reference

Current controls and tuning locations — 25 September 2026.

Controls and collection

WASD moves, mouse/wheel controls the camera, Space carries bridge blocks, E deposits one particle into the nearest unobstructed receiver within 1.5m, R returns loose tiles near their tree, Tab/K opens upgrades, and Esc opens settings. Walk within 1.1m of yellow particles to collect them. Inventory is shared across islands, capacity 30. Pause and focus loss prevent collection. Respawns take 30 simulation seconds.

Receiver rewards

Each tree has blue, violet and amber receivers. Three yellow particles deposited into one receiver produce a tile of that receiver's color. Charges are independent. Output is FIFO, and full receivers retain charge until a tile can appear. Returning a tile refunds three particles only if all three fit.

Dark opaque floor

Edit Assets/LightingBlocks/Art/Lighting/Materials/Platform0.mat, Platform1.mat and Platform2.mat. Shader: URP Lit; base color #14161B; metallic 0; smoothness 0.15; emission off; opaque, depth-writing surface. Player/tree lights illuminate it. Persist regenerated defaults in LightingSceneBuilder.PlatformMaterial.

Visibility

Select Soul Bridge Systems → Lighting Fog. World Clear/Black Distance defaults to 6/12m; Particle Clear/Hidden Distance to 4/6m. Particle Color defaults to #FFD66B and controls both base color and emission, fading to black. Distances are horizontal from the player; zoom cannot reveal distant particles.

Receiver placement and particle count

LightingParticles.cs defines ReceiverSpacing=8, ReceiverClearance=2.2 and DepositRange=1.5. ForestWorld's configuration controls island particle counts; Enable Lighting Particles controls generation. ForestWorld.InventoryCapacity is 30.

Scene regeneration

Tools → Lighting Blocks → Build Lighting Scene recreates Lighting from authored assets and builder defaults. Save manual tuning separately before rebuilding.

Particle pickup chime

Select Soul Bridge Systems → Lighting Pickup Audio in Lighting, outside Play Mode. The four Clips slots hold the original short crystal chimes. Drag a replacement WAV into a slot to change its sound. Files live in Assets/LightingBlocks/Resources/Audio/LightingPickups. Keep replacements short, with a gentle attack and no long echo; use Decompress On Load, PCM and Preload Audio Data.

Only successful collection plays the new sound. Full inventory, duplicate pickups, deposits, loading and respawning are silent on this channel. Four pooled voices cap overlap; pause and focus loss reset the sequence. The supplied WAVs peak at 0.36, leaving headroom for overlapping pickups. Recreate them with python Tools/audio/build_pickup_audio.py. Rebuilding the scene restores the four resource clips; keep permanent custom replacements in those files or update the builder's clip assignments.

Save compatibility and charge fixes

Lighting particle schema version 3 stores each reward's color and whether it consumes receiver charge. Migrated, already-earned tiles never consume new or partial charge. Full receivers retain charge while outputs are blocked and dim immediately after their own tile is created. Reloads rebuild the particle fog cache even when particle counts are unchanged.

Unspent old inventory becomes shared inventory, including balances above 30. Extra collection resumes after spending below the cap. Older colored deposits retain their color. Legacy scenes retain their own inventory, collection sound and save format. Previously lost resources cannot be reconstructed reliably.
